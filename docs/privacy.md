**Last Updated 20-11-2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information the "Vodič kroz Valjevo" app (the "app") handles, why, and what choices you have. The app is developed by Andjelka Dzida ("we", "us").

## Summary

- You can use the app without an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, or phone number.
- The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
- The only information we receive from you is what you send in a bug report, including your device model, operating system, and operating system version.

## Information Provided by You

The app has a bug report form. When you choose to send a bug report, we receive:

- the title and description you write;
- a file or screenshot, if you choose to attach one.

The title, description, and attachment are free-form: we do not ask for personal information in them and the form is not intended for it. Please do not include personal information about yourself or other people. You are responsible for anything you choose to enter or attach, and we are not responsible for personal details that you include in these fields. Anything you do include is stored as part of the report and can be deleted at your request, as described below.

## Information Collected Automatically

When you send a bug report, the app adds the following details about your device to the report so that we can reproduce and fix the problem:

- device model (for example, the model name of your phone);
- operating system (Android or iOS);
- operating system version.

These details are shown to you in the bug report form before you send it. They are read only for the bug report and are not sent at any other time. They do not include your name, your location, your contacts, or a unique device identifier.

## Location

The app does not collect, store, or send your location to us. When you ask for directions to a place, the app opens the maps application you choose on your device, and that application handles your location under its own privacy policy.

## Local Storage Notice

Your settings, such as the chosen language and script, are stored directly on your device. We do not use cookies, and these settings are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Use of Information

We use the information from bug reports only to:

- investigate, reproduce, and fix problems in the app;
- improve the app and its content.

We do not use this information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

## Third-Party Access to Your Information

The app relies on the following service providers. As with any internet connection, they receive technical data such as your IP address when the app contacts them.

- **Back4App** hosts the guide content and photos shown in the app and stores the bug reports you send, including the device details and attached files.
- **OpenStreetMap** provides the map images shown in the app. Map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers when you view a map.
- **External applications** such as your maps application or web browser are opened when you ask for directions or follow a link.

We do not share bug reports with anyone else, unless we are required to do so by law.

## Terms of Use and Privacy Policy of Third Parties

You acknowledge that the app uses third-party services which we do not manage or secure. Their handling of data is described in their own terms and privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Bug reports are kept so that we can investigate and fix the reported problem and maintain the app. You can have a bug report deleted at any time by sending a request to [andjelkadzida@gmail.com](mailto:andjelkadzida@gmail.com). Because reports are not linked to your identity, please describe the report (for example its title and the date you sent it) so that we can find it.

Sending bug reports is optional. If you do not send one, we receive no information from you.

## Children's Privacy

The app is a general tour guide and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Ongoing Amendments

We may modify this Privacy Policy. Changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date above will change. Your continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
